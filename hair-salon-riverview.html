Hair Salon for Riverview, FL

We’re not in Riverview. We’re ten minutes away, and plenty of our regulars make that drive every six weeks.

Usually it’s for color. Riverview has no shortage of salons that will cut your hair well. Fewer have a certified master colorist who trained at Redken’s 5th Ave Salon in Manhattan and Keune’s School of Color in Amsterdam — and who will tell you honestly when what you’re asking for will damage your hair.

Why people drive over from Riverview

Color worked out first

Every appointment starts with a consultation. We look at your hair in natural light, ask what has been on it before, and agree the result before anything is mixed. Ten minutes of driving is worth less than a color you have to live with for three months.

Correction is our favorite work

Brassy box dye, uneven banding, blonde that went orange. It is rarely a one-step fix, and we will say so up front rather than promise it in one sitting.

The same stylist, for years

One of our guests put it better than we could: “Micah has been coloring and cutting my hair for over 20 years.” That is the real reason people keep driving.

What we do

Four things, each at master level, each starting with a real conversation about the hair you want.

Color

Balayage, highlights and foils, corrective color, gloss and toner.

Treatments

Keratin and Brazilian Blowout, Olaplex and K18 bond repair, AI scalp analysis.

Cuts

Precision cuts for women, men, teens and kids, cut for the routine you really have.

Styling

Blowouts, special-event styling, updos and extensions.
