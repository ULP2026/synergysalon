2. Appointments and booking

  • Appointments can be booked online through our website or by calling or texting 813.654.2055.
  • An online booking is a request for a time. Your appointment is confirmed when you receive a confirmation from us.
  • You may request a specific stylist, or book the first available. If you do not request someone by name, we will assign an available stylist from our team.

3. Cancellations and late arrivals

Your appointment reserves a stylist’s time, so we ask for at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. You can cancel by phone, by text or by replying to your confirmation.

If you arrive late we will do our best to carry out the full service, but we may need to shorten it or rebook you, so that guests booked after you are not kept waiting. Repeated missed appointments without notice may mean we ask for confirmation before booking you again.

4. If something is not right

We make it right. If you are not happy with your result, tell us. Come back within 7 days of your appointment and your stylist will adjust it, complimentary.

Because every visit starts with a consultation, and nothing irreversible happens until you and your stylist agree on the plan, surprises are rare to begin with.

This applies to the service we performed. It does not cover a change of mind about a look you approved, work carried out by another salon, or a result affected by home colouring or treatments applied after your visit. We do not offer refunds on completed services.

5. Pricing and payment

  • Prices depend on the service, the length and condition of your hair, and the time and product required. We will quote you at your consultation, before any work begins.
  • Colour corrections and longer or thicker hair may take more time and product, and may cost more. We will always tell you before we start.
  • Payment is due at the end of your appointment.
  • Gratuities are appreciated but never expected.

6. Services, results and your safety

Hair is individual. Its history, texture, porosity, and any previous colour or chemical treatment all affect what can be achieved, and how the result looks and lasts. A photograph you bring in is a guide, not a promise.

So that we can work safely, please tell your stylist about:

  • any allergies or sensitivities, including to hair colour, or a previous reaction to a salon service;
  • any recent colour, keratin, relaxer, perm or home treatment, including box colour;
  • any scalp condition, medical condition, medication or treatment that may affect your hair or scalp;
  • if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

If you have not had a colour service with us before and are concerned about a reaction, ask us about a patch test before your appointment. We rely on the information you give us, and we may decline a service if we believe it is not safe for your hair or scalp.

7. AI scalp analysis

Our scalp analysis is a cosmetic assessment. It uses a digital scan to look at the surface of your scalp and hair, and it helps us recommend salon treatments and products. It is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace advice from a doctor or dermatologist. If you are worried about hair loss, a scalp condition or your health, please speak to a medical professional.

8. Children in the salon

For everyone’s safety, children who are not receiving a service need to stay with the adult who brought them, as our work areas contain hot tools and chemicals.
