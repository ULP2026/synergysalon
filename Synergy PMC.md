## Problems and Pain Points

**Core problem:** She wants to look and feel her best, and she does not trust just anyone with her hair. In a market with 33 competing salons within a few miles, she cannot tell who is actually skilled from a website. Picking wrong costs her money, weeks of bad hair, and sometimes real damage.

**Why the alternatives fall short**
- **Chains and discount salons:** cheap, fast, inconsistent. Whoever is available cuts your hair. No relationship, no expertise, high turnover.
- **Independent suites:** you might get a great stylist, but you are alone in a small room, there is no team to consult, no backup if she is booked or leaves, and no shared standard of quality.
- **Box color at home:** cheap up front, expensive to fix. This is the direct feeder into our corrective color business.
- **A friend who does hair:** convenient and awkward. Hard to be honest about the result, no recourse if it goes badly.

**What it costs her:** money spent on a result she hates, weeks of hiding her hair, real damage that takes months to grow out, and time wasted salon-hopping.

**Emotional tension:** anxiety about sitting in a stranger's chair and handing over control. Fear of walking out looking worse. Embarrassment about a color mistake. Underneath it all, the quiet feeling of not looking or feeling like herself anymore.

## Offer and Risk Reversal

**Flagship promise:** We make you feel your best.

### PROHIBITED CLAIM (locked, do not reintroduce)

> **The money-back guarantee is permanently retired.** It must not appear in any page, ad, email, text, social post, listing, signage, print piece, or AI-generated copy, ever again.
>
> **Banned phrasings, including all close variants:**
> - "Like it or don't pay"
> - "Love it or don't pay"
> - "If you don't like it, you don't pay"
> - "Your best haircut, or it's free"
> - "Love your hair or it's free"
> - "Satisfaction guaranteed or your money back"
> - "100% money-back guarantee"
> - "No-risk guarantee," "zero risk," "risk free"
> - Any construction where the price, the payment, or a refund is conditioned on the guest's satisfaction
>
> **Reason (locked):** the offer was systematically abused. Guests claimed dissatisfaction after receiving full services in order to avoid paying, repeatedly. The exposure is unbounded, it scales with the marketing, and the client has already been hit multiple times. Left in place it is a bankruptcy risk. This is not a wording problem to be solved with better fine print. The mechanism itself invites the abuse, so the mechanism is gone.
>
> **Anyone writing for Synergy, human or AI, should treat this block as a hard constraint and check copy against it before publishing.**

### What replaces it

The guarantee was doing real work: it answered every anxiety on the switching list. Deleting it without a substitute costs conversions. But the substitute must not be redeemable for money, because cash is what the abuse was after. A redo is worthless to a scammer and genuinely reassuring to a real guest.

**Recommended replacement, pending owner approval:**

**"We make it right."** If you are not happy with your result, come back within 7 days and your stylist will adjust it, complimentary.

Why this works:
- It removes the cash incentive entirely. Nobody runs the scam for a free second appointment.
- It is capped and known. The cost is one stylist's chair time, not the ticket plus the product plus the chair time.
- It is the industry-standard policy, so it reads as professional rather than desperate.
- It keeps the stylist accountable to her own work, which is what the original was for.
- The 7-day window is itself a filter. A real guest notices within days. Patterns of abuse tend to surface later.

**The stronger play: move the reassurance earlier.** The guarantee tried to fix anxiety at the end of the transaction. The consultation fixes it at the beginning, and costs nothing.

- **Complimentary consultation, always, before anything irreversible.** "Nobody touches your hair until we agree on the plan." This answers "what if it comes out worse" better than a refund ever did, because she never reaches the bad outcome.
- **The $40 AI scalp analysis** answers "will they actually know what they're doing" with a diagnostic no competitor has.
- **Forty-five years and 250+ reviews** answer "can I trust them" with evidence instead of a promise.
- **Corrective color as a named specialty** signals that we are the people who fix what went wrong elsewhere.

That stack is stronger than the old guarantee, and none of it can be gamed.

> **Owner decisions still needed:** approve or amend the redo policy wording, the 7-day window, and confirm how it is handled operationally between salon and stylist.

**Current promotions:** none. The salon does not discount and does not want to. This is correct and on-brand.

**Open to, and recommended:**
- A referral program (owner has already flagged this as a known gap)
- Value-add rather than price-cut offers: complimentary AI scalp analysis, complimentary consultation, a bond treatment included with color, new-guest gift
- Slow-day incentives, if any exist at all, aimed only at Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday
