1Overview

SynergyApp is the salon’s own system for running the day. The team signs in at app.synergysalon.com to see the appointment book, look after clients, set who offers which services and when, and keep track of what the salon has sent to clients.

It works hand in hand with the salon’s website, synergysalon.com. A guest who presses Book Now on the website is booked straight into the same appointment book the team works from, so there is one diary and nothing to copy across.

Who uses it

WhoHow they use SynergyApp
The salon’s owner and adminsRun the whole salon: every appointment and client, the team, the salon’s profile, automations and finance.
Stylists and the rest of the teamSign in to see their own appointments and clients, set their own services, prices and photo, and connect their Google Calendar.
ClientsNever sign in. They book on the website, receive emails, can text or call Karen, and change or cancel from the link in their emails.

3User Roles and Permissions

Everybody on the team has one role, chosen in their team member window under Personal information, Role. The roles are Owner, Admin, Manager, Front desk and Stylist. Owner and Admin have the same rights; the difference is only the title.

RoleSeesCan manage
Owner, AdminThe whole salon: every appointment, client and stylist, every message sent, and the Salon tab.Everything, including adding, changing and removing team members, their roles, services and hours; the salon’s profile; switching automations on and off.
ManagerTheir own appointments and clients, and the whole salon’s Finance on the Money tab.Their own account, services and hours; can also delete clients and appointments.
Front deskTheir own appointments and clients, and their own figures on the Money tab.Their own account, services and hours.
StylistTheir own appointments and clients (those booked with them, or that they added), and their own figures on the Money tab.Their own account, services, prices, available hours and Google Calendar.

Everyone can read the Team list (names, pictures and roles), but only an Owner or Admin can change somebody else. Anyone who is not an Owner or Admin sees Salon, Team, Automations, Money and Karen AI as their own share, read-only where it belongs to the whole salon: they can read the Salon Profile but not change it.

New team members

A new team member is added from Settings, Team, Add Team Member. Karen emails them an invitation with a one-time link, and they choose their own password. A new stylist is then taken through setting up their account (their details, services and prices, and hours) before the rest of the app opens.

4Booking and Appointments

Every appointment, however it was made, is in the same appointment book. The app only offers times when the stylist is working and free, and the same time can never be sold twice.

How appointments are made

WhereWhoHow
The websiteGuestsBook Now at the top of every page of synergysalon.com opens the booking survey. Under it, Earliest Appointment shows the first open time.
Appt. BookThe teamNew appointment, or click an empty time on the day view.
KarenClients and the teamBy text to Karen’s number. She offers real free times and books, moves and cancels.

Booking online (guests)

What brings you in?→ Who would you like to book with?→ Who is this appointment for?→ Service details→ Choose your availability→ Your information→ Book Me Now!
  1. The guest picks one or more kinds of service (Select all that apply).
  2. They choose a stylist, or the first available.
  3. They say who the appointment is for, and answer a question about each kind of service they picked.
  4. They choose a day and a time from the stylist’s real free times.
  5. They give their name, email and phone, with optional notes, and press Book Me Now!
  6. The confirmation reads You’re booked with the day, time and stylist, and the booking email follows.

Several services in one visit are booked as one appointment with one stylist who offers them all, long enough for everything.

Booking in the app (the team)

  1. Press New appointment on Appt. Book.
  2. Who is the stylist? Choose a stylist, or First available.
  3. What type of service? then Which service? Use Add another service for more than one.
  4. When? Pick a day and one of the free times.
  5. Who is the client? Type three letters of the name to find an existing client, or enter a new name, phone and email, with optional notes.
  6. Press Book appointment.

Help me book, at the top of the same window, hands the booking to Karen: she asks what is missing, one step at a time, and books it.

The appointment window

Click any appointment to open it. It shows when, the stylist, any processing time, the client’s phone and email, notes, who booked it, the services and a Payment panel. The buttons along the top:

ButtonWhat it does
Check inThe client has arrived. It then becomes Done, for when the visit is over; Undo check-in takes it back.
Move Appt.Choose a new day, stylist and time from the free times for the whole visit.
Cancel Appt.Cancels the appointment and frees the time.
No ShowMarks that the client did not come.
Delete TicketRemoves the appointment entirely.

Rescheduling and cancellations

When the team moves an appointment, the app asks how to tell the client:

  • Send the Client a Notification: the client is sent their new date and time.
  • Just Move the Appointment: the schedule changes and the client is not told.

Clients can change or cancel on their own. Every email they receive has a link to their appointment page, where Choose a new time shows the stylist’s free times and Move my appointment confirms it, and Cancel appointment cancels.

Appointment statuses

StatusMeaning
Booked / UpcomingBooked and still to come.
Checked inThe client has arrived.
DoneThe visit is finished.
No showThe client did not come.
CancelledCancelled by the salon or the client. The time is free again.

5Providers and Schedules

Stylists are the team members who take appointments. Each one’s window (open it from Settings, Team, or your own from My Settings) holds their picture, name and phone, Personal information, Connect your tools, Services & pricing and Available Hours.

Services & pricing

Each kind of work is switched on for the stylist who offers it, and opens to its services. Untick a service the stylist does not do; each one has its own price and its own minutes.

KindServices
CutsWomen’s Cut, Men’s Cut, Kids’ Cut, Bang Trim
TreatmentsKeratin & Brazilian Blowout, K18/Olaplex Treatment, Condition Treatment, Mask Treatment, AI Scalp Analysis
ColorColor, Partial Highlights and Full Highlights, each with its own times; then Balayage, Single Process Color, Biolage Highlights, Gloss or Toner, Corrective Color
StylingBlowout & Styling, Shampoo Blowout, Updos, Consultation

Color is timed in three parts: Start, Break and Finish, with the Total beside them. The Break is processing time, when the stylist is free to take somebody else, and the appointment book offers that time to other clients. A box at the foot, Special Instructions When Booking Clients, holds the stylist’s notes.

Start: the stylist applies the color→ Break: it processes, the stylist is free→ Finish: rinse, tone and style

Guests are booked for exactly the minutes the stylist has set. Left empty, the salon’s usual time for that service is used.

Available Hours

Available Hours are the times guests can book with this person. Switch each day on or off (a day switched off is closed), set its start and end, and use Add hours for a split day. Each day shows how much time it leaves bookable, and Total hours adds up the week.

The Lunch break is FLEXIBLE: 30 minutes between 11 AM and 1 PM, moved around bookings, so it never blocks a guest who needs that time.

Blocked time

Time blocked off for a stylist shows on the Appt. Book day view as Block, not bookable, and is not offered to anyone, online or in the app.

Online, a stylist is offered only once they have a service switched on, available hours set and their Google Calendar connected (see section 9).

6Client Management

Clients lists everyone who has booked or asked about a visit, newest first, with their status, phone, email, how many visits and their next appointment.

Finding a client

  • The search box at the top finds a client by name, email or phone.
  • Filters narrows the list by Status, Next visit (Has a visit booked or No visit booked), Tag and Card on file only. The button shows how many filters are on; Clear filters shows everyone again.
  • The sort list orders them by Recently added, Name A–Z, Name Z–A, Next visit (soonest), Last visit (most recent) or Most visits.
  • CHOOSE YOUR STYLIST narrows the list to one stylist’s clients, or All.
  • When booking, type three letters of a name in Who is the client? to find them.
  • Clients are added with Add client: name, phone, email, where they heard about the salon, and notes. A client who books online or through Karen is added on their own.

The client window

Click a client to open their window.

PartWhat it holds
HeaderTheir name and phone, their status, their tags, Need Help? (opens Karen already holding this client) and Book appointment.
Appt. BookVisits, Next and Last visit, and their recent appointments, each opening its window.
Contact detailsEmail, source, when they were added, and their stylist. The pencil edits them.
PaymentsThe Card on file switch (see section 7).
Contact preferencesWhether they are sent follow-up emails, and whether they have agreed to texts.
NotesAdd note for anything the team should know.
ActivityTheir history: appointments, emails, texts and changes to their details, filtered by All, Appointments, Email, SMS and Admin. A text or email can be sent from the foot of it.

Client status

The status in the header is one of New, Contacted, Booked, Client or Lost. Choose it from the list; booking moves a new client to Booked on its own.

7Payments and Card on File

SynergyApp does not take or record payments yet. Each appointment’s Payment panel says so; prices are kept on the services and the appointments so the salon can see what its visits are worth.

Card on file

The Card on file switch, under Payments in a client’s window, records that the salon holds a card for that client. It is a note for the team; the app does not store or charge the card.

Finance

Settings, Money opens Finance: what visits were worth, by stylist, for This month, Last month, Last 30 days or This year. Owners, Admins and Managers see the whole salon; everybody else sees their own.

FigureWhat it counts
EarnedThe price of the visits that have happened in the period.
Average visitEarned divided by the visits that have a price.
Booked aheadThe price of the appointments still to come.

Finance is worked out from each visit’s price as booked. It is what visits were worth, not money received. Cancelled visits and no-shows are left out, and a visit with no price counts as nothing until a price is added.

8SMS and Email

Automations

Settings, Automations lists the messages the salon sends on its own. Each one is switched on or off, and each opens to show its steps and the email itself.

AutomationSent when
Appointment BookingA client books an appointment
Appointment ReminderAbout 36 hours before an appointment

New Guest WelcomeSomebody books for the first time
No-show RecoveryStaff mark an appointment as a no-show
Late CancellationA client cancels close to the time
Post-visit Check-inAn appointment is marked complete

New Guest Second VisitA first visit finished and nothing is booked

Confirmations and reminders always go to a client with an email address. The follow-ups (thank-yous, rebook and win-back emails) can be switched off for one client under Contact preferences, Email.

Text messages

Texts come from Karen’s number. A client is sent appointment texts only once they have agreed to them, either by ticking the box at synergysalon.com/sms-opt-in or by texting START to Karen. Texting STOP ends them. Until a client agrees, their window says so and no texts are sent.

Writing to one client

At the foot of a client’s Activity, choose Text or Email, write the message and send it. A text goes from Karen’s number and their reply comes back to Karen; an email comes from the salon, and replies go to the salon’s reply inbox.

What was sent

Settings, Karen AI, Email History lists every email the automations have sent, newest first, with a search and a filter by kind of email. Each one opens the email as it was sent. A client’s own emails and texts are also on their Activity.

9Integrations and Administration

Connected services

ServiceWhat it does in SynergyApp
Google CalendarConnected by each stylist under Connect your tools. Their appointments are copied into their Google Calendar and kept up to date. Their own Google events count as busy, so online guests are not offered those times; on Appt. Book they show as Busy in Google.
Karen AIThe salon’s assistant, on her own phone number. She answers texts and calls (see below), and in the app through Call Karen and Need Help?.
EmailThe automations’ emails, sent from the salon’s own address.

Karen AI

Clients call or text Karen’s number to talk to her. She recognises clients and the team by the number they text from and greets them by name. For clients she books, moves and cancels appointments and tells them when theirs is; for the team she answers questions such as “What’s on my schedule tomorrow?”. A call is greeted and put through to the salon.

On the Karen AI tabWhat it shows
Karen’s numberHer phone number, and whether it is connected.
Chat HistoryThe conversations the team has had with Karen, in the app and by text.
Call HistoryEvery call to Karen’s number: who called, when, and whether the salon picked up.
Email HistoryEvery email the automations have sent (see section 8).
Where do you want people to reply toThe inbox client replies are delivered to. The Owner or an Admin sets it; once saved it shows Saved, with Change to set another.

Salon settings

On Settings, Salon. Everyone on the team can read it; only the Owner and Admins can change it.

  • Salon Profile: what clients see. The logo (Change logo, Remove), salon name, phone, website, Google review link, address, Hours of Operation and time zone. The logo is also the mark in the app’s menu.
  • Hours of Operation: each day Open with its opening and closing times, or Closed. They show on the website’s “Open today”, at the foot of every email, and to Karen. What a guest can book is still each stylist’s own Available Hours.

Managing the team

  1. Open Settings, Team. Each member shows their role and whether their calendar is connected.
  2. Press Add Team Member to invite somebody; Karen emails them and guides them through setting up.
  3. Click a member to change their role, services, prices and available hours, then Save.
  4. Remove from team takes somebody off for good; their past appointments keep their name.

Your own account

On My Settings: Edit my profile (picture, name, phone, password), Dark Mode, the app’s language, and Sign out.

Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page, press Forgot password? under the password.
  2. Type the email you sign in with and press Email me a reset link. The page says the same thing whether or not the address has an account.
  3. Open the email and press Choose a new password. The link works once, for one hour.
  4. Type the new password twice and press Save new password, then sign in with it. Anywhere still signed in with the old password is signed out.
